01  /  Around Ibiza

The White Isle

Cobblestone alleys. Salt-bleached walls. Plates of pan con tomate at harbor-side tables. Arriving in Ibiza is like stepping into a postcard soaked in golden hour. We landed, dropped bags at Nobu, and started exploring immediately — old town, Dalt Vila, hidden restaurants, the kind of sunsets you have to see in person to believe.

02  /  Nobu Hotel Ibiza Bay

Where We Stayed

Nobu Hotel Ibiza Bay was the anchor for the whole trip. Perched right on the water in Talamanca Bay, the pool looks out toward the old town and Dalt Vila fortress. Mornings started slow here — espresso by the water, rosé by noon. The kind of place that makes you want to extend your stay every single day. Our concierge Javier García made sure nothing was left to chance.

03  /  Nikki Beach Club

Nikki Beach

The definition of a perfect beach club day. White beds, cold drinks, music that builds from background groove to something you can't help but move to. Nikki Beach Ibiza sits right on the water — the kind of spot where afternoon becomes evening without you even noticing. We stayed far too long. No regrets whatsoever.

04  /  Swedish House Mafia @ Ushuaïa

The Reason We Came

This was it. The whole trip was built around this night. Swedish House Mafia — Axwell, Sebastian Ingrosso, Steve Angello — playing Ushuaïa Ibiza, the world-famous open-air superclub where the stage looks out over a rooftop pool and the Ibizan sky. Be there by 8pm. Ends at midnight. Four hours that felt like twenty minutes. One of those nights you'll talk about forever.

05  /  Amnesia Ibiza

Lose Yourself

If Ushuaïa is where you watch, Amnesia is where you disappear. One of Ibiza's oldest and most iconic clubs — a two-room beast where foam parties were invented and legends are made. Massive sound, laser grids overhead, bodies everywhere. There's a reason it's been in the DJ Mag top 10 for decades. We felt every single decibel.

06  /  Boat Day  ·  Beso Beach, Formentera

Six Hours on the Water

Arranged by our Nobu concierge Javier García, we chartered a boat for the day — 17 of us piling onto a vessel built for 25. The route: out of Ibiza harbor, across open sea to Formentera, the smaller wilder island to the south. Lunch was at Beso Beach — a terrace right on the sand, plates of grilled fish, salt-crusted bread, and views of water so clear it looks photoshopped. Six to eight hours total. Nobody wanted to come back.
