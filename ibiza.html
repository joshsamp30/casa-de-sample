Summer 2022  ·  Ibiza, Spain

IBIZA

The crew. The music. Eight hours on the water. One unforgettable week.

01  —  Nobu Hotel Ibiza Bay

CHECKED IN.

We landed, dropped our bags at Nobu, and didn't stop moving for five days. The pool looked like a postcard. We barely used it.

Day 1 · Niki Beach

02  —  Niki Beach

WHITE SUNBEDS. ALL DAY.

Rosé before noon. Music before that. Niki Beach does it like nowhere else — the kind of afternoon where no one checks the time and nobody cares.

Nobody left. Nobody wanted to.

03  —  Ushuaïa Ibiza

SWEDISH HOUSE MAFIA

Be there by 8. It ends at midnight. Swedish House Mafia. Open air. Four thousand people. One of those nights you don't sleep through.

Ushuaïa open air. The sound system hit the entire bay.

04  —  The Pardo · 8 Hours at Sea

FORMENTERA OR BUST.

Eight hours on a private boat. The water between Ibiza and Formentera is the clearest in Europe. We jumped in, we didn't want to get back out.

Formentera. The water doesn't look real until you're standing in it.

Bella's Beach House

05  —  Bella's Place

THE LAST MORNING.

Slow mornings at Bella's beach house. Between the cliffs and the sea. Cold drinks, nowhere to be, and everyone still going.

Five days. One week. Not enough. Ibiza has a way of making you feel like you've lived a whole summer in a single trip. We'll be back.
