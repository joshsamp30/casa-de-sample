July 7 – 8, 2022  ·  The Journey

LAX to
Santorini

Eleven time zones. Three flights. A red-eye layover in Montreal overnight. A turboprop from Athens — Sky Express GQ346 — with the island already visible through the window before the wheels touched down. White and blue and unmistakable.

By the time they landed at Santorini Airport it was 13:45 on July 8th. The driver — @klsbulldog — was waiting at arrivals, sign in hand. Sixteen minutes of winding caldera road separated them from the rest of it. Josh had been carrying the ring for months.

Friday, July 8, 2022  ·  Oia, Santorini

Canaves Oia
Epitome

Their suite: the Hideaway Pool Villa. Seventy square meters. King-size bed. Private pool. Open-air dining area. The caldera laid out beyond the edge. The kind of room that makes everything feel possible.

Chapter One  ·  Friday Night, July 8

The Letter
& The Outfit

An invitation delivered to the door. A gift waiting on the chair. She had no idea.

The night before, a letter arrived at the door.

She read it at the desk by the window, the lights of Oia still glowing outside. An invitation for a sunrise hike the next morning — the Oia to Fira trail. Nine kilometers along the caldera rim. Josh had written it like a suggestion. He had written five drafts before this one.

With the letter, a gift. A Prada gym outfit — chosen specifically for her, folded and placed on the chair. The cover story for the morning was a fitness class. Barry's-style. Organized by The Proposers. Something active and early, the kind of morning Danielle would never question. The outfit made it look like a plan. It was one.

She had no reason to suspect. The letter read like a proposal of a morning, not a life. It was, of course, both.

Chapter Two  ·  Saturday, July 9 · Before Sunrise

The Morning

Barry's doesn't exist in Greece. So Josh built it.

The driver — @klsbulldog — arrived before the sun.

Anyone who knows Josh and Danielle knows this about them: fitness is non-negotiable. Danielle runs on Barry's Bootcamp. Early class. High energy. The treadmill and the floor. It is not a hobby. It is a lifestyle.

Barry's is not in Greece.

So Josh fixed that. He found a personal trainer on the island. He sent him Barry's class recordings. He bought a Barry's shirt online and had it shipped to Greece. The trainer wore it. And as the sun came up over Santorini, they did the class by the water — Danielle in the Prada outfit from the chair, both of them working through the session while the caldera glowed orange behind them. She had no idea it had been engineered for her. She just thought it was a great morning. It was.

After the workout, a hike along the beach. At intervals, small framed signs placed along the path — each one carrying a line from one of her favourite rom-coms. Lines she had quoted to Josh without ever knowing he had been collecting them. A trail of love letters she could read with her feet.

  • Barry's Bootcamp recreated on the island · trainer sourced · shirt shipped from the US · class recordings sent
  • Workout by the water at sunrise · Danielle in the Prada outfit
  • Beach hike along Monolithos · volcanic black sand · framed rom-com signs placed along the path
  • Secret photographer and videographer in place the whole time

Chapter Three  ·  Saturday Morning · Monolithos Beach

The Breakfast

They rounded a bend. It had always been there, in theory. In practice, someone had built it in the dark.

They came around a bend in the beach and there it was.

A romantic bohemian picnic — set as if it had always belonged on that stretch of black sand. A handbuilt teepee draped in macramé and fresh flowers. Wood platters with pastries and fruit. Champagne already open. Moroccan cushions. Candles burning in morning light. A table so intentional it looked like a magazine spread. A team had built it in the dark hours before they arrived.

They sat together while the sun climbed behind the volcanic cliffs. The photographer was somewhere nearby — disguised as a fellow hiker, capturing every second she didn't know was being captured. This was one of those moments.

Chapter Four  ·  Saturday Morning · Monolithos Beach

The Ride

Up the beach. Down the beach. She almost got thrown. She passed the signs again. She had no idea what was coming.

After breakfast, horses were waiting.

They rode up the beach and back down — the volcanic cliffs on one side, the Aegean on the other. At one point Danielle's horse spooked. She nearly got thrown. Josh watched it happen and quietly kept it together. She held on. The photographer caught all of it.

On the way back down the beach, they passed the same rom-com signs from the hike. Same words. Different angle. A second chance to read them from horseback — and she still didn't know those signs were only part of the message.

The horses eventually brought them to the next stop. She still didn't know what the day was building toward.

Chapter Five  ·  Saturday Morning · The Treasure Hunt

The Treasure

A morning in Nashville. An idea that wouldn't leave. Three chests buried in Santorini. Metal detectors waiting.

This idea started in Nashville.

A few months before the trip, Josh and Nick and Danielle were sitting on a porch — hungover, the morning slow, the city still going somewhere below them. Across the way, a group of older men were out with metal detectors, working a patch of ground like they had all the time in the world. Methodical. Unhurried. Finding things. That image stayed with Josh long after the weekend ended.

He wanted to give Danielle a bachelor-style proposal day. Big moments. Real stakes. Something that felt like it was built specifically for her — not a single gesture but a whole production with chapters. The metal detectors were her chapter. A treasure hunt, built like the real thing.

Three chests were hidden in the volcanic terrain. Metal detectors were waiting when they arrived. She had to find them. That was the game. The stakes were higher than she knew.

Josh had placed the chests as a map of their story — where they had come from, where they stood at that exact moment, and where they were going. Not a gesture. An archive. Evidence of a relationship documented with intention.

The first chest held the past. A Wynn casino chip — and a piece of bread. Their first date was at Jardin in Las Vegas. Early morning. Danielle would only eat bread that day, watching what she had, because the pools were coming. Josh had to leave before it got late. He never forgot that morning. The chip was from the Wynn. The bread was the whole story. Neither of them needed a caption. She knew exactly what it meant. The second chest held the present. A Cartier pearl necklace. Chosen for the woman she had become. The third chest looked forward. A custom artwork: Josh and Danielle, Greece in the background. Because this island would always belong to them.

By the time the third chest opened, she was certain the moment had arrived. The day would continue. There was always going to be more. That restraint is what made the ending matter.

Chapter Six  ·  Saturday Morning · Into the Afternoon

The Afternoon

Back to the hotel. Massages. Then Ammoudi Bay — lunch with the Aegean at their feet.

By 09:15 the morning was done. The driver — @klsbulldog — brought them back to Canaves Oia Epitome.

They had already done more before noon than most people do in a week on holiday. An hour and a half later, a knock on the door. Couple massages — booked and ready. Another piece of the day Josh had arranged months before they ever landed on the island.

Then lunch. Private transfer to Ammoudi Bay — the tiny fishing port tucked below Oia at the base of the volcanic cliffs. 300 steps down, the sea right at the table edge. Lunch at Ammoudi Tavern Restaurant. Traditional. Quiet. The water so clear the boats look like they're floating on glass.

The afternoon had its own rhythm. The evening had not yet announced itself.

Chapter Seven  ·  Saturday Evening · Pyrgos Village

Pyrgos

The second photoshoot location. Volcanic stone. Golden hour. Two people carrying a secret hours from becoming permanent.

After Imerovigli they transferred to Pyrgos — the hilltop village at the centre of the island. Medieval streets. Volcanic stone walls. The highest point on Santorini, with the caldera spread out in every direction.

The photographer was still close. This was the second location — golden hour, 18:30, the light doing exactly what Santorini light does at that hour. Some of the most extraordinary frames from the entire trip were captured here.

She thought this was the point of the evening. She was one hour from Venetsanos Winery. She didn't know that yet.

Chapter Eight  ·  Saturday Evening · Hair, Makeup & Imerovigli

The Setup

A photoshoot that was never just a photoshoot. While she smiled for the camera, they built the rest.

Back at Canaves Oia Epitome, a makeup artist knocked on the door.

The cover story: a sunset photoshoot was planned. Josh had also arranged dresses — choices waiting in the room, something elevated to match the evening. She had no reason to question it. It was exactly the kind of thing a trip like this called for.

They were driven to Imerovigli — the quietest of the three caldera villages, perched at the highest point of the caldera rim. The photographer framed every shot. The afternoon light was exact. This was the first location. Pyrgos would follow. She was photographed before the story changed permanently.

What she didn't know: while she stood against Cycladic white-wash and blue dome, a team was at work at Venetsanos Winery. A large heart being assembled — pink florals, her favourite. Built floor to edge, bloom by bloom. Around the full perimeter, hundreds of candles and tealights being placed, one by one. A table set for the occasion. Musicians warming up to play one song. She was being photographed. He was watching the clock. The island was holding its breath.

Chapter Nine  ·  Saturday, July 9, 2022  ·  Sunset

The
Moment

Pink florals. Candlelight. One question. Yes.

At 19:45, they left Pyrgos. The transfer brought them to Venetsanos Winery — perched above the caldera at sunset.

First: a wine tasting. Not ordinary bottles. Each label had been custom designed — photographs of Josh and Danielle on the glass. Names from the places they had traveled together. The moments that quietly defined them as a couple, printed and poured. She had no idea what was waiting on the other side of that room.

She walked through a mirrored aisle. Fairy lights overhead. A chandelier above the table. Ahead: a large heart built entirely from pink florals — her favourite, her colour, every bloom placed for this exact evening. Around the full perimeter, hundreds of candles and tealights. Gold light everywhere.

Kygo's Unforgettable began to play. Live musicians. The song that had belonged to them long before this night had a name for itself.

The photographer was positioned. The videographer was rolling at ground level. A drone was above the island.

And Josh got down on one knee.

She said yes.

They toasted with champagne while the caldera glowed beneath them. Had a three-course dinner at Venetsanos Winery — the first meal as Josh and Danielle, engaged.

Later that night: Sphinx Wine Restaurant. Meal Two. The same cliffside table in Oia where the trip had begun to take shape. This time, everything was different.

The kind of evening that doesn't need embellishment. It needs only to be preserved exactly as it was. For the generations that follow.
