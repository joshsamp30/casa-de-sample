Chapter One

The Morning

Sunrise hike & a breakfast she didn't see coming

It started the way the best adventures do — with the open air of Santorini at sunrise and a trail carved into the cliffs above the caldera. The hike was Josh's first act. Disguised as a morning adventure, it was actually a carefully crafted stage set.

Hidden along the path, tucked into the volcanic rock and wildflowers, were signs. Each one carried a line — a quote from a love story that had long been theirs. A trail of breadcrumbs that only Danielle would understand.

  • Hidden signs with rom-com quotes along the cliffside trail
  • Secret photographer and videographer disguised as fellow hikers
  • The hike ended at a private terrace overlooking the caldera
  • A bohemian breakfast spread — champagne, fruit, flowers — waiting in the sun

Chapter Two

The Adventure

Above the Aegean & along the cliffs

After breakfast, the day shifted into something cinematic. A helicopter lifted them above the island — the white-washed cliffs and deep-blue sea reduced to a painting below their feet. Or perhaps horseback through Santorini's volcanic terrain, finding a secluded stretch of beach that felt like the edge of the world.

This was the exhale before the crescendo. Time suspended somewhere between sky and sea, shared with no one else.

  • Private helicopter flight over the Santorini caldera
  • Horseback ride through the volcanic landscape to a secluded beach
  • Aerial drone footage captured from above
  • A moment of just the two of them — before everything changed

Chapter Three

The Treasure Hunt

Past, present & future — wrapped in three chests

Three chests. Three chapters of their story — already written, and not yet begun. Josh laid them out as a map of everything they had built and everything that was still ahead of them.

The first chest opened onto their past — tickets back to Las Vegas, where it all began. The second held a present: a Cartier pearl necklace, chosen for the woman she had become. The third looked forward — a custom piece of artwork celebrating Greece, the country that would soon mean everything.

  • Chest 01 — The Past: Las Vegas tickets, where the story started
  • Chest 02 — The Present: Cartier pearl necklace
  • Chest 03 — The Future: Custom Greece-inspired artwork
  • Each chest was a chapter. Each gift, a sentence in a love letter.

Chapter Four

The Setup

A photoshoot that was never just a photoshoot

Late afternoon. Danielle was told a makeup artist was coming to prepare her for a photoshoot. A simple, beautiful cover story — one that let Josh ensure she'd be at her most radiant for the most important moment of their lives.

They walked together to the iconic blue-domed church of Santorini as the golden hour began to stretch across the caldera. She thought it was for photos. She had no idea what was being built around the corner.

  • Makeup artist arranged for the "photoshoot" cover story
  • Photo session at the blue church domes — iconic Santorini
  • Meanwhile: a large heart of pink florals, candles, and tealights was being assembled
  • Photographer and videographer positioned and ready

As the sun began its descent into the Aegean, Josh led Danielle to the proposal site. Surrounded by a heart-shaped arrangement of pink florals, hundreds of candles, and tealights casting a golden glow across the Santorini cliffside — he got down on one knee.

Kygo's "Unforgettable" began to play. A live musician filled the evening air. The drone hovered silently overhead. The photographer caught every second. And the rest of the world simply stopped.

  • Large heart installation — pink florals, candles, tealights
  • Live musicians performing Kygo "Unforgettable"
  • Professional photographer + videographer capturing every angle
  • Drone footage from above the caldera at golden hour
  • Champagne toast under the Santorini sky
  • Dinner under the stars with caldera views

Chapter Six

The Celebration

An enchanted dinner & the people who raised them

The evening didn't end at yes. It opened into something even more beautiful — an intimate dinner in Ibiza, surrounded by the nearest and dearest, to mark the beginning of what comes next.

Candlelight, ornate florals, the sound of the sea somewhere nearby. The kind of dinner you feel in your chest long after the plates are cleared. A table set for the people who helped build the love that was just celebrated.
