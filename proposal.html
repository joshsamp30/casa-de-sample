July 9, 2022  ·  Santorini, Greece

Friday, July 8, 2022  ·  Arrival

Canaves Oia
Epitome

It started in Los Angeles. Two flights, a layover in Montreal, an overnight to Athens, and one final hop on Sky Express GQ346 — and then the island appeared below the clouds. White and blue and unmistakable. By the time Josh and Danielle landed at Santorini Airport, they had crossed eleven time zones. Every mile of it was worth it.

A VIP driver was waiting at arrivals, sign in hand. Sixteen minutes of winding cliffside road, and then: Canaves Oia Epitome. Their suite perched high above the caldera with views that stop you mid-sentence. That evening, dinner at Sphinx Wine Restaurant — rooftop terrace, candlelit, the Aegean sun dissolving into the horizon below their feet. Neither of them knew the next evening would change everything.

Chapter One  ·  Saturday, July 9

The Morning

A sunrise hike, hidden signs, and a breakfast she'll never forget

A letter arrived at the hotel room the night before. An invitation to wake early and hike the Oia to Fira trail — the most scenic path on the island, nine kilometers carved into the caldera rim. Josh had arranged designer sportswear for Danielle to find waiting for her, so she'd look as effortlessly stunning as the landscape around her.

She had no idea the trail had been prepared for her. Tucked into volcanic rock and wildflowers along the cliffside path, Josh had hidden small signs — each one carrying a line from her favourite rom-coms. Lines she knew by heart. "You had me at hello." A trail of love letters she could read with her feet.

The hike ended at a private terrace above the caldera — a rustic bohemian breakfast spread laid out in the morning sun. Wood platters, champagne, fresh flowers, Moroccan cushions. And somewhere nearby, a "tourist" couple with cameras capturing every second she laughed and had no idea.

  • Invitation letter delivered to the room the night before
  • Designer sportswear arranged for Danielle to find on the morning
  • Hike: Oia to Fira trail · 9km along the caldera rim · ~3 hours
  • Hidden rom-com quote signs along the cliffside path
  • Bohemian breakfast setup: champagne, fresh fruit, flowers, Moroccan cushions
  • Secret photographer + videographer disguised as fellow hikers

Chapter Two  ·  Saturday Afternoon

The Adventure

Helicopter over the caldera, or horseback to the sea

After breakfast, the day shifted into something cinematic. Two options awaited — both ending at the same secluded beach, both beginning with something Danielle had never experienced.

A private helicopter lifted them above Santorini — the white-washed cliffs and deep blue caldera reduced to a painting below. Or perhaps it was horseback, riding through the island's volcanic terrain along the black sand coastline until the sea opened in front of them, quiet and entirely their own.

Either way, it was the exhale before the crescendo. Time suspended somewhere between sky and sea, with nothing but the sound of the Aegean and the two of them.

  • Option A: Private helicopter flight over the Santorini caldera
  • Option B: Horseback ride through volcanic terrain to the coast
  • Both routes end at a secluded private beach
  • Drone footage captured from above

Chapter Three  ·  The Beach

The Treasure Hunt

Three chests. Past, present & future — and a beautiful fake-out

On the beach, three chests were waiting. Josh had hidden them as a map of their entire story — where they had come from, where they were standing, and where they were going.

The first chest held the past — tickets back to Las Vegas, the city where it all began. The second, the present — a Cartier pearl necklace, chosen for the woman she had become. The third looked forward — a small custom artwork of the two of them, Greece in the background, because they would keep coming back.

By the time the third chest was open, Danielle surely thought the moment had arrived. And then — the day continued on. There was more. There was always going to be more.

Chapter Four  ·  The Afternoon Continues

The Wine &
The Water

Massages, custom bottles, and a message on the cliffs

The afternoon unfolded at a pace that only Santorini permits. Massages arranged near the secluded beach — the kind that dissolve the concept of time entirely.

Then came the wine tasting. But these weren't ordinary bottles. Each label had been custom designed: photographs of Josh and Danielle, their faces printed on the glass — and names for each bottle pulled from the places they had traveled together, the moments that had quietly defined them as a couple.

If it was on a boat, something even more extraordinary waited. As they drifted along the caldera cliffs, a message appeared on the rock face above the waterline — carved into the island itself. Four words that Josh had arranged to be displayed: "I will not give up on you."

Chapter Five  ·  Back at the Hotel

The Setup

A photoshoot that was never just a photoshoot

Back at Canaves Oia Epitome, a makeup artist knocked on the door. The cover story was simple: there's a photoshoot planned before sunset. Josh had also arranged for stylish dresses to be waiting for Danielle to find as part of the makeover — every detail considered, every inch of her experience curated.

They walked together to Santorini's iconic blue church domes — the photoshoot was real. The photographer was capturing it all. What Danielle didn't know was that she was also being photographed for the last time before becoming engaged.

Meanwhile, at the private proposal location, the final touches were being made. A large heart installation was being built — pink florals, her favourite, hundreds of candles, tealights. A table set for the occasion. And the musicians, warming up to play one song.

Chapter Six  ·  Saturday, July 9, 2022  ·  Sunset

The

Sunset, pink florals, one question — and yes.

At sunset, Josh led Danielle to the proposal site. As they arrived, she was guided to the center of a large heart — thousands of pink florals, her absolute favourite, surrounded by hundreds of candles and tealights casting gold light across the Santorini terrace above the caldera.

Kygo's "Unforgettable" began to play — live musicians filling the evening air with the song that had been theirs long before this moment. The photographer was positioned. The videographer was rolling. The drone was in the sky above the island.

And Josh got down on one knee.

She said yes. They toasted with champagne under the Santorini stars, the caldera glowing beneath them, and had dinner for the first time as Josh and Danielle — engaged.

Chapter Seven  ·  Ibiza · July 16, 2022

The Celebration

Amante Ibiza. Dinner for ten. The people who helped build this love.

The story didn't end at yes. It opened into something even more beautiful. One week after the proposal, on July 16th, the ten people who mattered most gathered at Amante Ibiza — a clifftop restaurant perched above the Mediterranean, where the sea meets the sky and the light turns gold at dusk.

Dinner for ten. The nearest and dearest. A long table overlooking the water, candlelit, draped in flowers, Spanish touches woven throughout the setting. The kind of dinner you feel in your chest long after the plates are cleared — the laughter, the tears, the stories, the toasts. Everyone leaving wanting more and already excited about the wedding day ahead.
