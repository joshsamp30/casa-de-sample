July 7 – 8, 2022  ·  The Journey

LAX to
Santorini

Anyone who knows Danielle knows this about her. She believes in love the way other people believe in facts. The Bachelor. The rom-coms. The grand gesture. Not as entertainment — as a standard.

Josh knew this. He had known it for years. So when it came time to propose, a dinner and a ring was never going to be enough. She had spent years watching strangers get the proposal of a lifetime on television. He decided she would get one that was real.

The proposal didn't begin at Venetsanos Winery. It began the second the wheels touched down in Greece. Every hour of July 8th and 9th was part of it. A 24-hour production — built from scratch, over six months — designed for one person, for one day that would never come again.

Danielle slept on the flight. Josh carried the ring. When the island came into view — white cliffs, blue water, nothing else — @klsbulldog was already waiting at arrivals. The plan was in motion before she even knew she'd landed.

Friday, July 8, 2022  ·  Oia, Santorini

Canaves Oia
Epitome

Some choices are decisions. Others are statements. Canaves Oia Epitome was a statement.

#1 Resort Hotel in Europe. #1 in Greece. 7th best hotel on the planet — Travel + Leisure World's Best Awards. Condé Nast Traveler Gold List. The kind of recognition that doesn't come from marketing. It comes from being genuinely irreplaceable.

Carved directly into the caldera above Ammoudi Bay. Volcanic stone. Endless sky. The Aegean below every terrace. The property sits at the edge of something — and you feel it immediately.

The Hideaway Pool Villa. A private pool at the caldera rim. The kind of room that makes the rest of the world feel very far away. Four nights. One of them would change everything.

Friday Night  ·  First Dinner Out

Sphinx Wine
Restaurant

The first night set the standard.

Sphinx Wine Restaurant has been in Oia since 1983. Not a trendy opening. Not a pop-up. An institution — built into the stone of the village over four decades and still the room everyone wants a table in.

A cellar with over 300 labels. Greek and international. Fifty poured by the glass. Mediterranean cuisine built around what the island produces and what the Aegean provides. Rooftop terrace. The caldera at sunset below their feet.

Josh had been here the night before with a ring in his pocket and twenty-four hours of planning still ahead of him. Danielle had dinner on a terrace in Santorini and thought the trip had already delivered. She had no idea it hadn't started yet.

Chapter One  ·  Friday Night, July 8

The Letter
& The Outfit

Josh wrote five drafts. The sixth one was left on her pillow.

The night before, Josh left a letter at the door.

He had written it five times. Not because the words were hard to find. Because they had to be exactly right. A letter that said everything without saying too much. An invitation that felt like a suggestion. It was neither.

On the chair beside it: a Prada gym outfit. Josh had chosen it himself. Her size. Her style. Something she would put on without thinking twice and feel exactly like herself in. The story he needed her to believe was simple — an early morning, a workout, a beautiful day ahead. She believed it.

What she didn't know: six months of planning had already moved through this room. The ring was hidden. The day was built. Every hour of July 9th had been placed in sequence — and she was about to step into it thinking she was just getting dressed for a workout.

The letter read like the beginning of a morning. It was the beginning of the rest of her life. She didn't know that yet. That was the point.

Chapter Two  ·  Saturday, July 9 · Before Sunrise

The Morning

Barry's doesn't exist in Greece. So Josh built it.

The driver — @klsbulldog — arrived before the sun.

Anyone who knows Josh and Danielle knows this about them: fitness is non-negotiable. Danielle runs on Barry's Bootcamp. Early class. High energy. The treadmill and the floor. It is not a hobby. It is a lifestyle.

Barry's is not in Greece.

So Josh fixed that. He found a personal trainer on the island. Sent him Barry's class recordings in advance. Bought a Barry's shirt online and had it shipped to Santorini. The trainer wore it. And as the sun came up over the caldera, they worked through the session together — Danielle in the Prada outfit from the chair, both of them moving while the water turned orange behind them. She had no idea it had all been engineered for her. She just thought it was a great morning. It was.

After the workout, they jogged along Monolithos beach. At intervals along the path — small framed signs, each carrying a line from one of her favourite films. Lines she had quoted to Josh over the years without ever knowing he had been collecting them. A trail she could read with her feet. Every sign placed by Josh. Every line chosen.

Chapter Three  ·  Saturday Morning · Monolithos Beach

The Breakfast

They rounded a bend. It had always been there, in theory. In practice, someone had built it in the dark.

They came around a bend in the beach and there it was.

A bohemian picnic — set as if it had always belonged on that stretch of black sand. A handbuilt teepee draped in macramé and fresh flowers. Wood platters with pastries and fruit. Champagne already open. Moroccan cushions. Candles burning in morning light. A table so intentional it looked like a magazine spread. A team had built it in the dark hours before they arrived.

She didn't see it coming. You never do when it's done right. One moment, a beach walk. The next — this. The volcanic cliffs behind it. The black sand beneath. The sun just clearing the ridge.

They sat together while the morning moved around them. The photographer was somewhere nearby — disguised as a fellow hiker, capturing every second she didn't know was being captured. This was one of those moments. The kind that only exist once. The kind worth building from scratch.

Chapter Four  ·  Saturday Morning · Monolithos Beach

The Ride

Up the beach. Down the beach. She almost got thrown. She passed the signs again. She had no idea what was coming.

After breakfast, horses were waiting.

They rode up the beach and back down. The volcanic cliffs on one side, the Aegean on the other. At one point Danielle's horse spooked. She nearly got thrown. Josh watched it happen and quietly kept it together. She held on. The photographer caught every second of it.

On the way back down the beach, they passed the same rom-com signs from the morning hike. Same words. New angle. A second chance to read them — this time from horseback. She still didn't know those signs were only part of the message.

It was already 09:00. The day had barely started. The horses brought them to the next stop. She still had no idea where it was all going.

Chapter Five  ·  Saturday Morning · The Treasure Hunt

The Treasure

A morning in Nashville. An idea that wouldn't leave. Three chests buried in Santorini. Metal detectors waiting.

It started with a note left on the beach.
Then a metal detector appeared in her hands.
What followed was twenty minutes of confusion,
two encounters with trash, and three buried chests
— followed by the greatest fake-out of the entire day.

The Note

After the horses came back down the beach, the driver pulled over. A sealed envelope was waiting. Not at a restaurant, not at the hotel — on a stretch of open volcanic terrain, nothing around them but black sand and sky.

She opened it. Inside: instructions. A metal detector was placed in her hands. She looked at Josh. He looked back. This was happening.

Armed & Hunting

She swept the volcanic terrain in the Prada outfit from the morning. The detector swept left and right. The ground gave nothing away. The photographer was somewhere close — still invisible, still capturing everything.

The Aegean was behind her. Somewhere beneath the surface, three boxes were buried. She had no idea what she was looking for yet. She just knew she was looking.

The False Starts

The first hit came fast. The detector went off. She got down, reached into the volcanic ground — and came up with trash.

She tried again. Hit number two: more trash.

She stood up. She looked at Josh. She was not remotely amused.

The volcanic ground of Santorini, it turns out, is full of things that are not treasure chests.

Chest One

The Past

Then the detector screamed. She could feel it was different this time. She dug. The box was real.

Inside: a Wynn casino chip. And a piece of bread.

Their first date was at Jardin in Las Vegas. Early morning. She was watching what she ate — the pools were coming. Josh left before it got late. He had remembered every detail of that morning since the day it happened. The chip was from the Wynn. The bread was the order she wouldn't finish.

Neither of them needed a caption. She knew exactly what it meant.

She kept going.

Chest Two

The Present

The second chest held a Cartier pearl necklace.

Not a placeholder. Not a symbol. The real thing — chosen for who she had become in the years since Las Vegas.

She didn't know there was a third box yet.

Chest Three

The Future

The third chest came up from the volcanic ground like it had been waiting there for years.

Inside: a custom painting. Josh and Danielle. Greece behind them. Not a photograph — a piece of artwork made for them, of them, on this island.

Because this island would always belong to them.

She dug it out of the ground herself.

The Reckoning

She carried all three back to the breakfast spot and laid them out in front of her. The chip. The bread. The necklace. The painting.

Past. Present. Future. A map drawn entirely in things he knew she would understand.

She counted. Three chests. No fourth box. She went through the logic again. Slowly. She was absolutely certain the ring was in there somewhere.

It wasn't.

She had approximately ten more hours left in the day.
She had no idea.

Chapter Six  ·  Saturday Morning · Into the Afternoon

The Afternoon

Back to the hotel. Massages. Then Ammoudi Bay — lunch with the Aegean at their feet.

By 09:15 the morning was done. The driver — @klsbulldog — brought them back to Canaves Oia Epitome.

They had already done more before noon than most people do in a week on holiday. A Barry's class. A hike. A bohemian picnic breakfast. Horseback riding. A treasure hunt with three buried chests. All of it before 09:00. All of it captured. None of it expected.

An hour and a half later, a knock on the door. Couple massages — booked and ready. Another piece of the day Josh had arranged months before they ever landed on the island. The afternoon belonged to them. Quiet. Intentional. The kind of break that makes the next moment feel even more earned.

Then lunch. Private transfer to Ammoudi Bay — the tiny fishing port tucked below Oia at the base of the volcanic cliffs. 300 steps down from the village. The sea right at the table edge. Lunch at Ammoudi Tavern Restaurant. Traditional. Quiet. The water so clear the boats look like they're floating on glass.

The afternoon had its own rhythm. The evening had not yet announced itself.

Chapter Seven  ·  Saturday Evening · Pyrgos Village

Pyrgos

The second photoshoot location. Volcanic stone. Golden hour. Two people carrying a secret hours from becoming permanent.

After Imerovigli, they transferred to Pyrgos — the hilltop village at the centre of the island. Medieval streets. Volcanic stone walls. The highest point on Santorini, with the caldera spread out in every direction below.

The photographer was still close. Always close. This was the second photoshoot location — golden hour, 18:30. Santorini light at that time of day does something unusual. It lands sideways. Warm. Long shadows against white stone. Some of the most extraordinary frames from the entire day were taken here.

She thought this was the point of the evening. A photoshoot at golden hour in a medieval hilltop village. That's already remarkable. She was satisfied. Content. She was also one hour from Venetsanos Winery. She didn't know that yet.

Chapter Eight  ·  Saturday Evening · Hair, Makeup & Imerovigli

The Setup

A photoshoot that was never just a photoshoot. While she smiled for the camera, they built the rest.

Back at Canaves Oia Epitome, a makeup artist knocked on the door.

The cover story: a sunset photoshoot was planned. Josh had also arranged dresses — choices waiting in the room, something elevated to match the evening. She had no reason to question it. It was exactly the kind of thing a trip like this called for.

They were driven to Imerovigli — the quietest of the three caldera villages, perched at the highest point of the caldera rim. The photographer framed every shot. The afternoon light was exact. This was the first location. Pyrgos would follow. She was being photographed before the story changed permanently.

What she didn't know: while she stood against Cycladic white-wash and blue dome, a team was at work at Venetsanos Winery. A large heart of pink florals — her favourite — being assembled bloom by bloom. Around the full perimeter, hundreds of candles and tealights placed one by one. A table set for dinner. Musicians warming up for one song.

She was being photographed. He was watching the clock. The island was holding its breath.

Chapter Nine  ·  Saturday, July 9, 2022  ·  Sunset

The
Moment

Pink florals. Candlelight. One question. Yes.

At 19:45, they left Pyrgos. The transfer brought them to Venetsanos Winery — perched above the caldera at sunset.

First: a wine tasting. Not ordinary bottles. Each label had been custom designed — photographs of Josh and Danielle on the glass. Names from the places they had traveled together. The moments that quietly defined them as a couple, printed and poured. She had no idea what was waiting on the other side of that room.

She walked through a mirrored aisle. Fairy lights overhead. A chandelier above the table. Ahead: a large heart built entirely from pink florals — her favourite, her colour, every bloom placed for this exact evening. Around the full perimeter, hundreds of candles and tealights. Gold light everywhere.

Kygo's Unforgettable began to play. Live musicians. The song that had belonged to them long before this night had a name for itself.

The photographer was positioned. The videographer was rolling at ground level. A drone was above the island.

And Josh got down on one knee.

She said yes.

They toasted with champagne while the caldera glowed beneath them. Had a three-course dinner at Venetsanos Winery — the first meal as Josh and Danielle, engaged.

Later that night: Sphinx Wine Restaurant. Meal Two. The same cliffside table in Oia where the trip had begun to take shape. This time, everything was different.

The kind of evening that doesn't need embellishment. It needs only to be preserved exactly as it was. For the generations that follow.
